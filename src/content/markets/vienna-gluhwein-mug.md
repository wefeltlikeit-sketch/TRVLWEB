Vienna's Christmas markets run on a quiet, elegant piece of engineering: the *Pfand*. You pay a few euros' deposit on a decorated ceramic mug with your first Glühwein, and at the end of the evening you face a choice — return the mug and get your coins back, or simply... not.

Nobody minds. The mug is the souvenir programme. Each market designs its own, dated by year, and the Rathausplatz mug — boot-shaped, absurd, wonderful — is the classic.

## Practical notes

The mug wants to travel in the middle of the bag, wrapped in the scarf you bought at the same market (this is also the scarf-justification system working as intended). Ceramic survives a carry-on without drama.

One warning from experience: decide early which market's mug you're keeping, or you will arrive home with five of them, a jingling bag, and no coins left for the airport trolley.
